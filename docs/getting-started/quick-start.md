# [Documentation](https://outletpro.zip/documentation/)

Outlet Pro allows your WooCommerce store to have a dedicated outlet page for
listing overstocked and discontinued products.

## Getting started

To set up your store with Outlet Pro, first **tag your outlet products** and then
**publish the outlet page**.

---

### Name your outlet

The name of the outlet appears in the page title and navigation.

1. In **Pages**, locate the page labelled **Outlet page** and click **Edit**.
2. Open **Page settings** and select the **Page** tab.
3. Click the pencil icon (**Rename page**) beside the current name and enter your
   outlet's name, such as "Clearance".
4. If needed, click **Slug** to change its URL, such as `clearance`.
5. Click **Save**, review the selected changes, and confirm the save.

---

### Select outlet products

1. In **Pages**, locate the page labelled **Outlet page** and click **Edit**.
2. In the **Manage outlet products** panel, search by product name or SKU.
3. Use the toggle to include products in your outlet.
4. Click **Save**, review the selected changes, and confirm the save.

---

### Customize the outlet badge

The outlet badge applies to all outlet products, and is displayed across the
store on the product page, cart, and checkout.

1. In the outlet editor, open **Page settings** and select **Badge**.
2. Click the current **Design** and choose **Last chance**, **Clearance**,
   **Final sale**, or **Outlet**.
3. Use the controls to change label text, colors, typography, dimensions, or
   border. The preview shows your changes.

---

### Customizing the outlet message

The message applies to all outlet products and is displayed on the product page.

1. In the outlet editor, open **Page settings** and select **Message**.
2. Change the message text.

---

### Publishing the outlet page

1. Click **Publish** in the outlet editor.
2. Review the selected changes.
3. Press save.

If the page is already published, click **Save** instead.
