Move old stock easily by adding a dedicated outlet to WooCommerce.

== Description ==

Every store needs an outlet.

This extension adds a dedicated outlet to your store to help you sell old stock, so you can make way for newer and exciting products.

It gives you a simple, easy way to manage this:

* Include products in the stores’s outlet with a single checkbox
* A page listing the products in the stores’s outlet
* A customizable badge on products belonging in the stores’s outlet
* Policy messaging (e.g. no change-of-mind returns)
* Customize the name and messaging to suit your store (e.g. "Last chance", "Discontinued", "Reduced to clear")

Clear stock faster and help customers find it before it’s gone.

= What is an outlet? =

An outlet is a dedicated area to promote the products you need to clear. It helps customers easily discover products they might want before they are gone. Products in the stores’s outlet display a badge and message, allowing you to apply special policies such as no change-of-mind returns.

= How do I add products to the outlet? =

Add or edit a product, scroll to the "Product data" section, and under Inventory check "Include in outlet", then save.

= Is this the same as putting products on sale? =

No. This plugin introduces a dedicated outlet, which you can name anything you like. Use it to clear old stock and apply different policies to these products. Sales campaigns are better suited for store-wide or time-limited promotions.

= Where is the outlet on my store? =

A draft outlet page named "Outlet" is automatically created when the extension is installed. Update it as needed and publish it to make it visible on your store.
